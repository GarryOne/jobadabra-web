What the comparisons actually showed

  • Letters: Sonnet beat Haiku 13–7 on five cases across both judges and both answer orders.
  • Judge bias: on 15 letters, Opus beat Haiku 23–7 under the Opus judge, but only 16–14 under the Sonnet judge. The owner chose Sonnet.
  • Other steps: Sonnet won patterns; Haiku won several facts-focused reviews and summaries. Small samples and a facts-focused rubric do not prove one model is always better.
  • Monthly cost: no measured workload per Pro user, so no bill is claimed for 100 or 1,000 users. Per-call cost is not a monthly-user forecast.
