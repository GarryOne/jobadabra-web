How does the free trial work?

The whole app is free for your first 20 applications or your first 60 days, whichever lasts longer. Our AI is included, so there is nothing to set up. You can try Always on during the trial.

How do AI credits work?

Pro + AI includes €10 of AI credit, about one whole job search. Credit is used at our AI prices: €1 of credit buys €1 of our AI. If you search heavily, top up with €5, €10 or €20, and each adds what you pay (€5 adds €5 of credit); each top-up is a one-time payment. The AI credit never expires and there is no renewal.

Free AI is included in the trial, nothing to set up. Add your own Claude or ChatGPT key if you want stronger letters and interview prep: your own key is unlimited and billed by your provider.
