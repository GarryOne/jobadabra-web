Where the others are the better choice

Any job, anywhere

Simplify fills forms on over 100,000 career sites and matches you to millions of openings. Jobadabra searches any role across Europe, and fills the big application systems best.

Volume

If you want hundreds of applications a day, JobCopilot and LazyApply do that. Jobadabra won't: it's built for fewer, better applications that you check.

CV templates

Teal and Huntr have polished resume builders with many templates. Jobadabra tailors the CV you already have, in its own design or a clean default.

No setup

The others are a sign-up away. Jobadabra needs a Mac or Windows computer, a free Notion account (Free AI needs no key): about ten minutes, guided by the app.
