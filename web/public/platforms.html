Platform support

Will it work with my setup?

Check your computer, browser, AI and the accounts you already use.

Desktop app

A native app for macOS and Windows.

  • MacApple Silicon · M1 or later (not Intel)
    Supported
  • WindowsWindows 10 or 11 · 64-bit
    Beta

The Mac app isn't signed by Apple yet: your Mac asks once to confirm. Windows installs from the Microsoft Store, with no warning.
