What these numbers can tell you

These are benchmarks we run ourselves, not an independent certification or a promise about every job site.

Blind judging
The judge sees the answer or listing and the supplied facts, without the tool's name. Search verdicts separate role and location; form verdicts separate right, wrong and empty.
Search limits
Different source coverage and small samples matter. Role match is not application success. “Both” requires role yes and place yes; remote and unknown location verdicts are kept separate. This snapshot does not measure all jobs available on the market.
Form limits
One repeat per tool, six forms, one fictional applicant and Haiku 5.5 on CI. Model access and tool execution differ. A single repeat gives no estimate of run-to-run variation. It is not a production-wide success rate.
Newer attempts
Two later post-fix rounds captured all contestants, but judging stopped at a service usage limit. We keep the last completed scored round visible, with its build and date, until those captures have valid scores.
Check the evidence
The downloadable snapshot contains aggregate counts, fictional persona labels, the scored run ID and source hashes. It contains no applicant answers or personal data. This is a dated snapshot, not a live counter.
